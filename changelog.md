# Changelog

What's new in each version of **ParrotApp**. The newest version is at the top.

---

## [4.12.12] — 2026-10-05 _(current)_

- **Better lyrics in fullscreen.** Timed lyrics now show five lines at once, with the line being sung
  highlighted. Songs with only plain lyrics scroll slowly from start to end as the song plays.
- Lyrics you have marked as out of sync (key **0**) still show, but no line is highlighted.

---

## [4.12.11] — 2026-10-05

- Screenshots of music videos with very long names are now saved with a shorter file name, instead of
  failing.

---

## [4.12.10] — 2026-10-02

- **Fixed:** searching for a cover online now works for single songs too, not only for albums.

---

## [4.12.9] — 2026-10-02

- **Make a single song into an album.** You can now turn one song into an album of its own from the
  edit window.
- When songs are made into an album, or a song is taken out of an album, the picture stored inside the
  music file is used as its cover if there is no other cover.

---

## [4.12.8] — 2026-09-30

- **Fixed:** in Firefox, changing a song's Studio/Live or Source choice in the player sometimes did not
  save.

---

## [4.12.7] — 2026-09-30

- **No more duplicate uploads.** When you upload a song that is already in your library, it is skipped
  and you are told why.

---

## [4.12.6] — 2026-09-28

- **The automatic library scan waits until you are not using the app.** It starts only after a few
  minutes with no activity (you can change this in Settings), and never while music is playing or a
  radio recording is running.

---

## [4.12.5] — 2026-09-28

- **Clear message while a scan runs.** A banner at the top of the page shows when the library is being
  scanned. Music, photos and other library pages now tell you right away that a scan is in progress,
  instead of loading for a long time and then failing.
- A song that is already playing keeps playing during a scan.

---

## [4.12.4] — 2026-09-25

- The Help page and the user guide now explain how to create albums and how to use lyrics.

---

## [4.12.3] — 2026-09-24

- **Mark lyrics as out of sync.** Sometimes the lyrics found online belong to a different version of
  the song, so their timing is wrong. Press **0** in the player to mark (or unmark) them.

---

## [4.12.2] — 2026-09-24

- **Shuffle stays inside your filter.** When you narrow the list by clicking an artist, album, genre or
  year, shuffle now picks songs only from that list.

---

## [4.12.1] — 2026-09-24

- The **U** key now closes the song details window as well as opening it.

---

## [4.12.0] — 2026-09-21

- **Lyrics.** Press **Y** (or the new button in the player) to see the lyrics of the playing song. They
  are found online for free and saved, so each song is looked up only once. Use **Search again** if
  the lyrics are wrong.
- **Lyrics as subtitles.** In fullscreen, press **T** to show the lyrics line by line, in time with the
  music.

---

## [4.11.3] — 2026-09-21

  previous visit.
